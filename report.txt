Phishing Email Analysis Report
Cyber Security Internship – Task 2

Objective:
To analyze a suspicious email sample and identify phishing characteristics.

Sample Email Summary:
The email claims to be from a bank requesting urgent verification of account details due to suspicious activity.

Phishing Indicators Identified:
1. Spoofed sender email address (support@secure-bank-alert.com).
2. Urgent and threatening language pressuring immediate action.
3. Suspicious hyperlink redirecting to a fake login page.
4. Mismatched URL when hovered over the link.
5. Poor grammar and spelling mistakes.
6. Request for sensitive information such as password and OTP.
7. Email header analysis showed discrepancies in sender domain.

Header Analysis Findings:
The return-path domain does not match the displayed sender address. SPF and DKIM checks failed, indicating possible spoofing.

Risks Associated:
• Credential theft
• Financial loss
• Identity theft
• Malware infection
